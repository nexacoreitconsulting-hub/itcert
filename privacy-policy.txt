Privacy Policy — Civics 100

Last updated: [fill in the date you publish]

Civics 100 ("the app") does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share
any personal information.

What the app stores, and where
- Your "mastered" question checklist and your practice-quiz score history
  are stored only on your device, using Android's local app storage
  (SharedPreferences) and your browser's local storage if you use the web
  version.
- None of this data is sent to any server. The app has no backend, no
  account system, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.
- Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently, since it never
  existed anywhere else.

Third parties
- The app does not include ads, ad networks, or third-party analytics
  SDKs.
- The app does not share data with USCIS, the U.S. government, or any
  other party, because it does not collect any data to share.

Content source
- Civics test questions are sourced from official USCIS publications
  (Civics Questions and Answers, 2025 version M-1778 and 2008 version),
  which are U.S. government public-domain works.

Affiliation
- This app is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with,
  endorsed by, or sponsored by USCIS or the U.S. government.

Changes to this policy
- If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
  page with a new "Last updated" date.
